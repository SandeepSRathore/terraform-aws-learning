## Not verified by LocalStack (needs real AWS)
- The IAM role's s3:GetObject permission: LocalStack's metadata service has no credential endpoint, so the script uses dummy keys there.
- systemd: LocalStack instances are containers without an init system, so step 50 used its background-process fallback.
- Reaching the app from the internet: the security group isn't enforced, and the Mac can't route to container IPs.
